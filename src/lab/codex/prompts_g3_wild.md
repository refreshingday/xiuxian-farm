# g3_wild texture prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final PNGs are resized to 512 × 512. Texture channels are normalised by the lab (`norm: 0.9`) and multiplied by painted vertex colours.

1. `assets/textures/codex/g3_wild_bark.png`

   Seamless tileable texture, 512x512 PNG, straight-on orthographic view of chunky weathered tree bark for a cozy xianxia farming game. Hand-painted pixel-art style with soft painterly shading, colours #87735c to #ad9272. Broad soft vertical irregular bark furrows, occasional rounded knots, low contrast mid-tones suitable for tint multiplication. Even flat lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no text, no border. Edges must tile seamlessly on all four sides.

2. `assets/textures/codex/g3_wild_leaves.png`

   Seamless tileable texture, 512x512 PNG, straight-on orthographic view of dense overlapping small maple and ginkgo leaves for a cozy xianxia farming game. Hand-painted pixel-art style with soft painterly shading, colours #b99d62 to #d2b77c. Low contrast mid-tone leaf mosaic, broad readable lobed and fan shapes, no gaps or background, subtle veins suitable for tint multiplication. Even flat lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no text, no border. Edges must tile seamlessly on all four sides.
