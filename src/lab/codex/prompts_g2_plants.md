# g2_plants texture prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final PNGs are resized to 512 × 512. Both are opaque repeating surface textures; the lab normalizes their average colour before multiplying the model's vertex colours.

1. **g2_plants_bark.png**

   Seamless tileable texture, 512x512 PNG, straight-on orthographic view of warm smooth old orchard bark with broad gently winding vertical grain and sparse rounded knots for a cozy xianxia farming game. Hand-painted pixel-art style with soft painterly shading, colours #92704f to #b59a72. Mid-tone low contrast, evenly distributed detail, no deep black cracks. Even flat lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no text, no border. Edges must tile seamlessly on all four sides.

2. **g2_plants_foliage.png**

   Seamless tileable texture, 512x512 PNG, straight-on orthographic view of dense overlapping small rounded leaf clusters, a continuous foliage surface for solid tree canopy meshes in a cozy xianxia farming game. Hand-painted pixel-art style with soft painterly shading, colours #77925b to #a4b77d. Mid-tone low contrast, broad readable clusters, opaque full coverage, no holes, no branches. Even flat lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no text, no border. Edges must tile seamlessly on all four sides.
